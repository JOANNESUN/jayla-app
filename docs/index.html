Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Jayla is a baby-tracking app made by Joanne Sun. Your privacy, and your family's, is the whole point of how it's built.

What we collect

Nothing. Jayla does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server. There is no account and no sign-up.

Where your data lives

Everything you log — feeds, sleeps, diapers, and your baby's profile and photo — is stored only on your own iPhone. This data is included in your device's normal encrypted backups (iCloud or Finder) if you have those enabled; that backup is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Jayla.

Third parties

Jayla contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking software. No data is ever shared with anyone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Jayla schedules gentle reminders locally on your device. These are generated on your phone and are not sent through any server.

Children

Jayla is a tool for parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email joannecysun@gmail.com.

Because Jayla collects no data, there is nothing for us to sell, lose, or leak.
